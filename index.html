Frequently Asked Questions

Is i:ON Academy Right for My Child?

Who do you serve?

We serve children and young adults ages 2–21 with autism, ADHD, Down syndrome, developmental delays, and other behavioral, developmental, or executive functioning needs.

Our assessment helps identify your child’s strengths, needs, and whether our services are a good fit. If another service would be more helpful, we can guide you toward other options. The decision to begin services is yours.

I’ve heard concerns about ABA. What is your approach?

Your concerns matter. At i:ON Academy, we use compassionate, evidence- and assent-based care that respects your child’s voice, strengths, and individuality. We focus on skills that support communication, independence, relationships, and everyday life.

We welcome questions so you can decide whether our approach fits your family’s values.

Services & Insurance

Where are services offered?

We offer services at home, in our clinic, in the community, and at school when permitted. School support may include direct services, observations, or teacher consultation, depending on school policies.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept Blue Cross Blue Shield and Aetna. We are credentialed with Cigna but are not currently accepting it. Credentialing with Texas Medicaid and Medicare is in progress.

Private pay and out-of-network options may also be available. Contact welcome@ionaba.com to confirm availability and get help understanding your benefits.

Specialty Programs

Students must be learning in a general education setting to participate in our specialty programs.

What is Focus Lab?

Focus Lab is an in-person executive functioning program for students in grades 4–6 and high school. It builds organization, planning, homework, time management, and independence skills. No formal diagnosis is required.

What is PEERS®?

PEERS® is an evidence-based social skills program for teens ages 14–17 who communicate using spoken language and can participate in a general education group setting.

No diagnosis is required for private-pay enrollment; insurance coverage may require one.

Do you support children with Down syndrome?

Yes. We support communication, independence, social development, executive functioning, and everyday life skills. We strongly recommend collaborating with your child’s physical therapist.

For specialty program availability and enrollment, contact academy@ionaba.com.

Getting Started

How do I get started?

Request a free 30-minute consultation. If our services are a good fit, we’ll assess your child’s needs and begin insurance authorization, if applicable.

Once the assessment and any required authorization are complete, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan and schedule services.

During Services

How do parents participate?

A one-hour parent meeting is required each week. We also encourage parents to practice strategies between visits and share progress, questions, and concerns with the treatment team.

Can I observe in-home sessions?

Yes. We encourage parents and caregivers to observe sessions and learn strategies to use during everyday routines.

Can siblings participate?

Yes, when appropriate. Siblings can join activities that help your child practice communication, play, and social skills within family routines.
